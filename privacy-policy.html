Privacy Policy for School Management App

Effective Date: October 18, 2025

Welcome to School Management App (“we”, “our”, or “us”). We respect your privacy and are committed to protecting your personal information. This Privacy Policy explains how we collect, use, and protect user data within our app.

1. Information We Collect

  • Student name, class, roll number, and contact details
  • Teacher name, class handled, and login details
  • Performance data such as test scores, attendance, and results
  • Uploaded images or documents (stored securely in Firebase/Cloudinary)
  • Email ID and password (via Firebase Authentication)

2. How We Use the Information

We use this information only for educational and administrative purposes, such as:

  • Managing student registration and attendance
  • Recording and displaying student performance
  • Allowing teachers to upload and view results
  • Sending notifications related to academic activities

We do not sell, rent, or share your data with any third party.

3. Data Storage and Security

All user data is stored securely in Firebase Realtime Database and protected using authentication and encryption. Uploaded media are stored in Cloudinary. Only authorized users can access their respective data.

4. Children’s Privacy

Our app is designed for school use. We do not knowingly collect data from anyone under 13 years without parental or school consent.

5. Third-Party Services

Our app uses:

  • Firebase
  • Cloudinary

These third-party services have their own privacy policies that we encourage you to review.

6. Your Data Rights

You may request correction or deletion of your personal data by contacting the school administrator. You may also withdraw consent by uninstalling the app.

7. Changes to This Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. Any updates will be posted on this page with a new effective date.

8. Contact Us

If you have any questions or concerns about this Privacy Policy, please contact us at: school.app.support@example.com
