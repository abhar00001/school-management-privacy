1. Information We Collect

We may collect the following types of information:

Personal Information: Student name, class, roll number, and contact details.

Teacher Information: Name, class handled, and login details.

Performance Data: Test scores, attendance, and uploaded results.

Media: Photos or documents uploaded by teachers or admins (stored securely in Firebase/Cloudinary).

Authentication Data: Email ID and password for login (handled through Firebase Authentication).

2. How We Use Your Information

We use this information only for educational and administrative purposes, such as:

Managing student registration and attendance.

Recording and displaying student performance.

Enabling teachers to upload and view results.

Sending notifications related to academic updates or activities.

We do not sell, rent, or share your data with any third party.

3. Data Storage and Security

All user data is stored securely in Firebase Realtime Database and protected using authentication and encryption.

Uploaded media (like result photos) are stored securely in Cloudinary.

Only authorized teachers, admins, and the respective students can view their information.

We take appropriate security measures to protect your data from unauthorized access.

Our app is designed for school use.
We do not knowingly collect personal information from anyone under the age of 13 without parental or school consent.
